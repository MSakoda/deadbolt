Deadbolt privacy policy

Deadbolt is a small, private cooperative board game that a group of friends play by taking turns online. It is a hobby project, not a business.

What it stores

  • Your email address, your display name and your time zone, so you can sign in and get the right reminders.
  • The games you take part in: which survivors you control and every move you make.
  • A sign-in cookie in your browser so you stay signed in. There is no password.

How email is sent

Deadbolt sends you sign-in links, game invitations, turn reminders and a daily summary. These are sent from the game owner's Gmail account through the Gmail API. The permission requested is only “send email”. Deadbolt cannot read, search or delete anyone's email, and it only uses that permission to send the messages described here.

Who sees your data

Other players in a game can see your display name and what you do in that game. Your email address is shown only to the host of a game you are in. Data is kept in a hosted database and on a hosting service, and is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than running the game.

Deleting your data

Ask the person who invited you, or the owner of the game, to delete your account and your games, and it will be removed.
